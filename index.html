Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers about root canal procedures, clinic timings, and treatments.

Is Root Canal Treatment painful? +
Root canal treatment at Malathi Dental Clinic is performed under effective local anaesthesia. Modern endodontic techniques ensure that patients feel comfortable and relieved from pain rather than experiencing discomfort.
Can Root Canal Treatment really be finished in one visit? +
Yes. For clinically suitable cases without active periapical swelling, Dr. Malathi performs Single-Sitting Root Canal treatments. This saves you multiple visits, multiple injections, and gets you back to eating comfortably quickly.
Do I strictly need a crown after a Root Canal? +
In most cases, yes. After root canal therapy, the tooth loses its blood supply and becomes brittle over time. A custom-fitted dental crown protects the tooth from cracking under normal chewing pressures.
How do I know if my tooth pain requires a Root Canal? +
Classic warning signs include throbbing pain that wakes you up at night, prolonged sensitivity to hot or cold, discomfort when chewing, or tender gums. Dr. Malathi will conduct a thorough clinical evaluation and consultation first, recommending an X-ray only if strictly necessary to confirm the diagnosis.
Will I need an X-ray during my consultation? +
Not necessarily. Backed by over 15 years of seasoned clinical experience, Dr. Malathi conducts an in-depth physical examination and clinical analysis first. To protect patients from unnecessary radiation, X-rays are never done routinely — they are advised only when strictly required for precise diagnostic assessment.
Do you treat children and offer pediatric dental care? +
Yes, absolutely! Dr. Malathi specializes in gentle pediatric dentistry, treating children with great care, explaining procedures warmly, and ensuring fear-free visits for young patients.
What are your clinic timings and locations? +
Both our Maruthi Layout and Hosa Road clinics in Basapura / Electronic City operate Monday through Saturday from 9:30 AM to 7:30 PM. Sundays are closed.
