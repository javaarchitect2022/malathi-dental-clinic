Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about root canals, visits and timings.

Is root canal treatment painful?

With modern local anaesthesia and a gentle, unhurried approach, most patients feel little to no pain during the procedure — many say it feels like getting a filling.

Can my root canal finish in a single visit?

For clinically suitable cases, yes. After an X-ray and examination, Dr. Malathi will confirm whether your tooth qualifies — the focus is always on doing what's right for the tooth, not rushing.

How much will my treatment cost?

It depends on the tooth and what's needed. You'll always get a written estimate after examination, before treatment begins. Call or WhatsApp 99004 01661 for guidance.

Will I need a crown after the root canal?

Often, yes — a crown protects a root-canal-treated tooth and helps it last for years. Your plan will spell this out clearly upfront.

Which clinic should I visit?

Whichever is closer — Clinic 1 (Maruthi Layout) or Clinic 2 (Hosa Road / Gregorian Nagar). Just pick your preference in the booking form.

Are you open on Sundays?

No — Sunday is a holiday. We're open Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM to 7:30 PM. For Sunday tooth emergencies, WhatsApp us and we'll guide you.
