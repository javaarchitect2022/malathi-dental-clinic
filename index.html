Root Canal Treatment

When saving your natural tooth is possible, it is worth considering.

Root Canal Treatment is used to treat infection or inflammation inside a tooth and can often help preserve a natural tooth that might otherwise need to be removed.

At Malathi Dental Clinic, Root Canal Treatment is planned after proper examination and X-ray assessment. Single-visit treatment may be possible for clinically suitable cases.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers — anything else, call or WhatsApp us.

Is Root Canal Treatment painful?

Treatment is carried out under effective local anaesthesia — most patients report little to no pain.

Can Root Canal Treatment be completed in one visit?

In clinically suitable cases, yes — completed in a single sitting. Suitability is confirmed after examination.

Will I need a crown after Root Canal Treatment?

Often, yes — a crown protects the treated tooth. Your plan will make this clear upfront.

How do I know if I need a Root Canal?

Persistent tooth pain, prolonged sensitivity to hot or cold, swelling, or pain when chewing can signal nerve damage. Only an examination can confirm.

Do you treat children?

Yes — gentle dental care for young patients, alongside care for adults.
